# Game Day postmortem — 2026-11-08

## Summary

A solo local Game Day exercised worker loss, local Postgres loss, and synthetic
IAM/secrets response fixtures. All live faults were restored. Sale recovery met
the existing local targets: SC-01 RTO was 60 seconds; SC-02 was 30 seconds from
database readiness. SC-02 recorded 2,431 HTTP 200 application errors while the
database was down or becoming ready. Every acknowledged sale in both runs
matched one ledger row; no duplicates or lost responses were found.

## Impact and scope

No production service, identity, data, credential or notification was involved.
One local kind worker was killed for eight minutes. One local Supabase Postgres
container was stopped for eight minutes, then restored with its existing
volume. SC-05 and SC-06 used disposable synthetic fixtures. No recording was
made. All times and counts are bounded to this local session and the recorded
30-second measurement resolution.

## What happened

SC-01 ran from 09:03:10Z to 09:21:47Z. Its control passed before worker3 was
killed at 09:05:19Z. The worker returned at 09:13:24Z and was Ready at
09:13:27Z with its original IP. Client failures were sent from 0 to 49 seconds
after the fault; sale success recovered at 60 seconds. The ledger reconciled
5,465 validated sales with no lost acknowledgements, lost responses, duplicate
tags or unmatched rows.

SC-02 ran from 09:46:49Z to 10:09:25Z after a fresh 150-sale smoke. Its control
passed before the local DB stopped at 09:48:50Z. The DB was ready at 09:56:55Z.
Fault-relative recovery was 510 seconds; the independent DB-ready-relative
measurement was 30 seconds. Twenty failures were sent after the restore
command during the DB readiness transition. The final ledger reconciled 4,319
validated sales; 2,431 requests were HTTP 200 application errors. No
acknowledged sale was missing or mismatched, and there were no duplicates or
lost responses. No app replicas restarted. No firing alert series appeared in
the sampled ranges.

SC-05 and SC-06 synthetic rehearsals passed at 10:11:53Z. They do not establish
real AWS IAM/session enforcement, Gitleaks coverage or issuer-side secret
revocation. The closure check at 10:12:10Z found the local DB healthy, all four
kind nodes Ready and Tawira 3/3 Ready.

## Contributing factors and findings

- The business path can fail while returning HTTP 200. SC-02's sale UUID and
  ledger reconciliation were necessary to report outcomes accurately.
- Sale recovery and database readiness are different anchors. The 510-second
  fault-relative result includes the planned outage and transition; the
  30-second DB-ready result is reported independently.
- Twenty errors after the restore command demonstrate a readiness transition.
  The analyzer's complete-window rule avoids treating the first partial
  recovery window as sustained recovery.
- SC-02's sampled timeline had no firing alerts; SC-01 did not capture an
  alert-range export. Neither establishes notification delivery. The existing
  F-01 sale-detection gap remains open.
- The synthetic SC-05/SC-06 workflows rehearse response order only. They do not
  close the live-provider evidence gaps.
- A one-host kind cluster cannot prove independent zone behavior. The
  ADR-0005 placement overlay was not part of this Game Day.
