**Classification:** valid local database-outage observation
**Operator path:** `node scripts/sc02-db-loss.mjs --check`, fresh `sc02smk10` smoke, then `node scripts/sc02-db-loss.mjs --run`

## Scope

The selected local Supabase Postgres container was stopped for the declared
480-second hold and started again with its existing volume. Tawira replicas and
the load pod were not restarted. This is a host-level local fault, not a
hosted-Supabase or independent-zone result.

- Database stop: `2026-11-07T16:18:58Z`; `DB_READY`: `2026-11-07T16:27:02Z`.
- Fault-relative recovery: **510 s**, 30-second complete-window resolution.
- DB-ready-relative recovery: **30 s**, within the 300-second target.
- Reconciliation: 4,306 rows, 4,306 distinct tags, zero duplicates, zero unmatched tags and zero lost responses.
- No application restart occurred. The exported alert ranges contained no firing `ALERTS` or `HighLatencyP99` series.

## Interpretation

This repeat supports the database-failure and automatic-recovery hypotheses for
this local setup. It also reproduces the first run's recovery anchors while
having no lost response. The combined SC-02 evidence remains bounded by the one
lost response in `sc02b`; it does not establish universal retry safety or a
volume-loss RPO. The 29 requests sent after the restore command and before the
later readiness event are transition errors, not acknowledged sales.
